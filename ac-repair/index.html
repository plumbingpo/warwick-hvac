Warning Signs for AC Repair

The sooner you notice these red flags, the easier and cheaper the fix. Keep an eye out for the following clues:

  • Warm or room-temperature air blowing when the thermostat calls for cooling
  • The system short-cycling every few minutes without cooling the house
  • The outdoor unit humming or clicking but the fan not spinning
  • Hissing or bubbling sounds that suggest a refrigerant leak
  • A burning smell or repeated breaker trips when the AC starts
  • Cooling bills climbing while the house stays sticky and warm
  • Water dripping from the indoor unit or showing up on the ceiling below
  • Frost or ice forming on the copper lines or the indoor coil

At Warwick HVAC, each cooling call starts with measurement. Capacitors are metered against the rating on the label. Amp draws on the compressor and fan motor are compared to the data plate. Why It Matters

The Benefits of Professional AC Repair

Consider the refrigerant first. It lives in a sealed, pressurized loop, venting it is prohibited under federal rules, and correct service requires gauges, a vacuum pump, and a recovery machine rather than a store-bought recharge kit. Add capacitors that hold a dangerous charge with the power off and a condensing unit fed by 240 volts, and the risks add up quickly. Then there are the diagnostic traps: an iced coil often points to poor airflow rather than low charge, and topping off a system with an unrepaired leak lets moisture and acid slowly damage the compressor while your money escapes with the refrigerant.

A professional repair looks at the whole system, not just the symptom. Our technicians meter electrical parts before replacing them, make charge decisions from measured superheat and subcooling, locate leaks with electronic detectors and dye, and check the filter, blower, and static pressure any time airflow could be the issue. The result is a repair that lasts the season instead of a guess that fails in three weeks. If your system is not worth saving, you will hear it from us directly, backed by numbers rather than a sales pitch.

Homeowner Tips

AC Repair Prevention Tips

A few simple habits now, like regular filter changes and seasonal tune-ups, can add years to your system's life. Put these technician-approved steps to work around your property:

  • Rinse the outdoor condenser coil each spring and keep vegetation two feet back
  • Shade helps, but never enclose the outdoor unit; it needs free airflow on all sides
  • Start each cooling season by flushing the condensate line so algae cannot block it
  • Change the filter monthly during cooling season; starved airflow is how coils freeze
  • Schedule a spring tune-up so weak parts are caught before summer arrives
  • Keep supply and return vents open; closing rooms raises static pressure on the blower
  • Pay attention to buzzing or clicking when the AC starts, a common warning of a failing capacitor
