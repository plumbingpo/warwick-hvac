Clear Pricing

Clear Pricing Before Any Work Starts

Nobody likes a surprise on the invoice. So Warwick HVAC prices every Warwick job before the work begins, after a full diagnostic that looks at the equipment's age, fuel type, and tonnage, refrigerant levels, coil and heat exchanger condition, and the ductwork feeding each room. You'll know what's wrong, what it takes to fix, and what it costs before you say yes.

The problem with HVAC failures is that many of them develop out of sight. A pinhole leak in the evaporator coil bleeds off refrigerant, the blower bearings wear down gradually, and a weak capacitor holds on until the first hot afternoon, when the compressor won't start. When bills climb, airflow drops, or ice forms on the lines, get it looked at while it's still a simple repair.

Not every call is an emergency. Plenty of Warwick homeowners ask us to size up an aging system before it fails, and it's a smart move. With a typical AC or furnace lifespan of 12 to 18 years, replacing on your own schedule beats a rushed swap in the middle of a heat wave. We'll give you a straight answer on how much life your equipment has left.

Seasonal Tune-Ups

Seasonal HVAC Maintenance Plans

The best repair is the one you never need. That's the idea behind our seasonal plans. On each scheduled visit, we clean coils and burners, confirm the refrigerant charge, test the electrical components, inspect the heat exchanger, flush the condensate drain, and change the filter, all matched to the equipment in your home.

Most homes do best with two visits a year: a springtime tune-up for cooling and a fall tune-up for heating. The schedule flexes with the age of your equipment, how hard your household works it, and local conditions like dust and pollen, which clog condenser coils and raise energy use quickly.

Consider maintenance as a safeguard for equipment you've already bought. Each tune-up helps keep the coils clean, the charge correct, and the filter fresh, which lowers what you pay to run the system while easing the strain on the compressor and blower. For most households, the plan costs far less than an early replacement.

Right-Sized Systems

Equipment Sized for Your Actual House

Getting the size right is one of the most overlooked parts of any install. Before recommending new equipment, Warwick HVAC works through the details that drive your home's heating and cooling load: square footage, insulation, window exposure, and duct layout. That gives you a system that runs in long, efficient cycles, controls humidity, and keeps every room at the temperature you set.

Choosing new equipment is about more than a SEER2 number. We'll compare heat pumps against a furnace-and-AC pair, and ducted against ductless, laying out operating costs, electrical and venting work, and the installation itself. Families with a growing household frequently do best with two-stage or variable-speed equipment, which holds tighter temperatures across the whole house and uses less energy. You choose what fits your home and budget.

Frequent Failures

Heating and Cooling Problems We Solve in Warwick

The majority of service calls we take in Warwick come down to a handful of familiar problems. The air conditioner blows warm air. The furnace won't light, or it lights and shuts off minutes later. Ice builds up on the evaporator coil, the condenser hums without starting, or a mini-split starts flashing error codes. Add refrigerant leaks, clogged drains, and forgetful thermostats, and you have the full list. We fix every one of them, starting with the exact part that failed.

Some problems are mostly a nuisance, while others pose real risks. A weak capacitor simply keeps the compressor from starting, but a cracked heat exchanger can leak combustion gases into your living space, and a blocked condensate drain can quietly ruin a ceiling. We match the fix to the failure: capacitors, contactors, and fan motors on cooling systems, igniters, flame sensors, and control boards on furnaces, sealed and repaired ductwork where conditioned air is bleeding into the attic, and a proper refrigerant recharge after a repaired leak. Whatever you're facing, our team has the parts and experience to handle it.
